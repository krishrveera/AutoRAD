3. Equal-Power Audio Panning MappingYou cannot map a linear 0.0 to 1.0 ratio directly to left/right volume channels. If you do, when the car is perfectly centered (0.5 L and 0.5 R), the overall volume will actually sound quieter than when it is fully panned to one side. This is a known psychoacoustic phenomenon.You must map your smoothed RAD ratio using an Equal-Power Panning trigonometric curve. Assuming a RAD of 0.0 is the left boundary and 1.0 is the right boundary:$$Volume_{Left} = \cos\left(\frac{\pi}{2} \cdot RAD\right)$$$$Volume_{Right} = \sin\left(\frac{\pi}{2} \cdot RAD\right)$$
